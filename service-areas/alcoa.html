Why Alcoa Has Diverse Spray Foam Needs

Alcoa has two distinct housing eras and they need different things. The original 1914-1940 Alcoa company houses - the Bassel Heights and Springbrook Drive bungalows - have minimal cavity insulation, single-pane windows, and uninsulated crawl spaces with stone or early concrete foundations. These respond best to crawl encapsulation plus rim joist spray foam plus attic work, often as a phased project across 2-3 years. The newer Alcoa subdivisions - Topside Estates, Eagle's View, Lambert Acres, the developments off Wright Road - are typical 1990s-2010s post-frame and stick construction with attic ductwork. These need attic encapsulation primarily, often combined with rim joist work.

The commercial growth along Pellissippi Parkway and Cusick Road has also driven a lot of light commercial spray foam work - metal building insulation for warehouses, distribution facilities, and the auto repair shops that line the corridor. Our crew handles both residential and light commercial in Alcoa.
