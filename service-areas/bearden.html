Why Bearden Crawl Spaces Are a Spray Foam Specialty

Bearden has the oldest housing stock in our regular service zone. Many of the homes along Tallhassee, Wesley, Cherokee Boulevard, and the Sequoyah Hills streets were built in the 1920s-1940s on perimeter stone foundations with dirt-floor crawl spaces. These crawls have had a century of East Tennessee humidity working on them. We routinely find rotting wood floor joists, sagging fiberglass batts that have been wet since the 1980s, and active mold colonies on the underside of the subfloor.

Bearden crawl encapsulation is more involved than a newer-home crawl job. The substrate prep often requires removing decades of accumulated debris, treating mold, replacing rotted joist segments, and sometimes installing a perimeter drain before the vapor liner and spray foam can go in. We have the experience to do this right - hundreds of Bearden-area crawl projects under our belt - and the typical Bearden full encapsulation runs $7,500-$14,000 including prep. The comfort and utility savings are dramatic given the baseline these homes are starting from.
