Quick Answers

Is Gatlinburg in a colder code climate zone than Knoxville?

No. The energy code assigns zones by county, and all of Sevier County is Climate Zone 4A, the same as Knox County, even though the high ground around Gatlinburg runs colder. The town averages about 8 inches of snow a year. Code minimums are the same as in the valley, so going past them is your call.

Can you insulate a Gatlinburg cabin that stands on posts or pedestals?

Yes. When the underside of the floor is open to the weather, closed cell foam sprayed against the subfloor and joists is the usual answer, because it does not absorb water and also stops wind washing through the floor. Where foam stays exposed underneath, we discuss a protective cover or code-approved coating as part of the price.

Which building code does Gatlinburg use?

The City Commission adopted the 2018 International codes, including the residential code and the 2018 IECC, on October 20, 2020. They took effect November 4, 2020, and full compliance applied to applications filed after May 4, 2021. The City of Gatlinburg Building and Planning Department handles permits for work inside the city.

Building and Insulating in the Mountains

Higher, wetter, tighter lots

The town is hemmed in by high ridges, with the West Fork of the Little Pigeon River as its main watershed and US 441 running through the middle. About 58 inches of precipitation fall here in a typical year, against 51.93 inches at Knoxville's airport. Water running downhill toward a foundation is a constant on sloped lots, so under a Gatlinburg cabin we look at grading, gutters and soil moisture before we talk about any liner or foam.

More homes than residents

Gatlinburg has 4,830 housing units for 3,663 residents, and only about 55 percent of those units are detached houses. The 2020 census listed 65.8 percent of units as vacant, with a 31.7 percent rental vacancy rate, which reflects overnight cabin and condo stock rather than abandoned homes. Across the wider Gatlinburg and Pigeon Forge area, AirDNA counted almost 17,000 short-term rental units in April 2023, more than half of them cabins. For an owner, that means work has to fit between guests, and a building that sits closed for days needs moisture control that runs on its own.

The age of the housing, and the 2016 rebuild

The median home in Gatlinburg was built in 1987, and the 1980s alone account for 1,382 units, the largest single decade. A second era came after the Chimney Tops 2 fire. In November 2016 it destroyed 981 residential and 53 commercial structures inside the city, and 14 people lost their lives. Some Chalet Village homeowners rebuilt with fire-resistant concrete siding, steel supports, metal roofs and concrete pedestal foundations. The census now counts 541 units from 2010 to 2019 and 157 from 2020 on. On a home from that rebuild, the insulation is usually newer and closer to code, while the raised, open-underneath floor is what changes our approach.

Neighborhoods and landmarks

We work from Chalet Village on the ski mountain to Mynatt Park and out along the 8 mile loop of the Arts and Crafts Community. Ober Gatlinburg, the only ski resort in Tennessee, sits above the chalets, a reminder of how cold this end of the county can get.

Most-Requested Services in Gatlinburg
