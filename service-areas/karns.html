Quick Answers

Who issues building permits in Karns?

Knox County Codes Administration and Inspection, because Karns has no city government of its own. The county adopted the 2018 International Codes under Ordinance O-18-10-101 on November 19, 2018, for permits bought on or after January 1, 2019. The county codes site was down for maintenance when we checked in October 2026, so we confirm editions by phone.

How old are Karns homes?

Newer than Bearden's, with a gap in the middle. In the Karns census area, the median home dates to 1992 and about 39 percent predate 1980, including 279 homes from the 1960s. Across the wider 37931 ZIP the median is 1997, and about 44 percent of units were built in 2000 or later.

Are Karns and Hardin Valley the same for insulation?

Not quite. Both are unincorporated and fall under Knox County Codes, but Hardin Valley's 37932 ZIP is newer, with a 2004 median build year. Karns keeps a real stock of older homes, so its jobs lean toward attic air sealing, crawl space moisture control and, now and then, filling an empty wall.

Karns Homes and the Beaver Creek Valley

The Karns census-designated place is small, about 2.98 square miles of land with an estimated 4,115 residents. The 37931 ZIP that carries a Karns mailing address is far bigger, about 31,842 people across the Karns and Byington area. When we say Karns, we mean both.

1

Two building booms

Of 1,519 units in the census area, 279 date to the 1960s and 422 to the 2010s, with thinner building in the 1980s and 1990s. A 1960s ranch and a 2015 two-story need opposite things. The ranch usually wants attic air sealing, a deeper attic layer and a look inside the walls. The newer house usually has its walls covered and needs help at the bonus room, the attic hatch and the ducts.

2

Mostly detached houses, some manufactured homes

About 69.5 percent of the 13,373 units in 37931 are single-family detached, and 756 units, about 5.7 percent, are mobile homes. Inside the census area the detached share rises to about 85 percent. Manufactured homes need their own approach, under the floor and along the skirting, which our Powell page walks through.

3

Low ground along Beaver Creek

Beaver Creek, a Clinch River tributary, runs through Karns at roughly 960 to 1,000 feet, with ridges on both sides and Oak Ridge Highway down the middle. Creek-bottom lots hold moisture. DOE's crawl space guide says framing should stay below 17 percent wood moisture content, so we meter the joists before suggesting foam or a liner.

4

Named subdivisions

Karns Landing and Padgett Hill are newer construction, with listings around $520,000 for a four-bedroom Karns Landing plan and about $420,000 at Padgett Hill. Royal Oaks is a resale neighborhood of ranchers. A builder can often price an attic or wall upgrade before drywall; a Royal Oaks rancher is a retrofit from the attic and crawl.

5

County code and the eave detail

Knox County's amendments to the 2018 residential code kept the older minimums: attic R-38, walls R-13, crawl space floor R-19. The county's energy handout lets full-height, uncompressed R-30 over the top plate at the eaves count toward the R-38 attic. The eave is where attic insulation usually runs thinnest, so baffles and air sealing there matter.

6

A school town that kept growing

Karns High School opened in 1913 and is named for Thomas Conner Karns, Knox County's first Superintendent of Public Instruction. The community was called Beaver Ridge until residents voted for the Karns name in the 1950s. Growth crowded the school enough that Hardin Valley Academy opened in 2008 to relieve it, which matches the newer houses on the census tables.

Most-Requested Services in Karns

  • Crawl space vapor barrier: creek-bottom ground under Karns homes stays wet, and a sealed liner is the start of drying it out.
  • Attic insulation: the 1960s ranches have broad, shallow attics where a few inches of old fill sit under leaky ceilings.
  • Injection foam: some of those ranches still have empty or half-filled exterior walls, which we confirm with a test hole.
  • New construction insulation: subdivisions such as Karns Landing and Padgett Hill are still building, and an upgrade costs least before drywall.
  • Bonus room insulation: two-story homes from the 2010s often have a room over the garage that never matches the thermostat.
  • Rim joist insulation: in a damp valley, the band joist above the foundation is a cold, leaky spot where mold likes to start.
