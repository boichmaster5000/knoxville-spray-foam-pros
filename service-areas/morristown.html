Quick Answers

Do you really come out to Morristown from Knoxville?

Yes. Morristown is about 40 miles northeast of Knoxville by I-40 and I-81, and Hamblen County is one of the nine counties we cover. The estimate is free and happens at the property, the same as in Knoxville, because we need to see the attic, crawl space and walls before we can price anything honestly.

What code does the City of Morristown enforce?

The city's Building Inspections Department lists the 2018 International Residential Code, the 2018 International Building Code, the 2018 International Energy Code and the 2017 National Electrical Code. Under Tennessee's amended version of the 2018 energy code, a Zone 4 house needs R-38 in the ceiling, R-13 in framed walls and R-19 in floors.

Where does a 1960s or 1970s Morristown house lose the most heat?

Usually at the top and the bottom. The Department of Energy points to the attic and the crawl space or basement as the most important leak areas, and leaky ducts there can add a lot more. Morristown has 2,435 homes from the 1970s and 2,233 from the 1960s, and most of them deserve that check first.

Morristown Housing, Lakes and Climate

Incorporated in 1855, Morristown is the only incorporated city in Hamblen County, and parts of it reach into Jefferson County. The 2020 census counted 12,934 housing units with 8.4 percent vacant, so most homes here are lived in year round, unlike the tourist towns to the south. About 61 percent of units are detached houses and 815, roughly 6 percent, are mobile homes. About a third of all units went up in the 1960s and 1970s, decades before the state's current R-38 ceiling minimum.

Ridge and Valley country on Cherokee Lake

The city sits at about 1,302 feet along the Holston River and Cherokee Lake, with Clinch Mountain to the north and the Smokies on the southern horizon, in what locals market as the Lakeway Area. It gets about 44 inches of precipitation a year, and January highs average near 45 F, a few degrees below Knoxville's 48.2 F. Summers still peak near 85 F, so humidity and crawl space moisture belong in the same conversation as the heating bill.

Lake subdivisions

Bridgewater Pointe, a lake-access subdivision whose covenants allow only site-built homes, along with the Timbercrest lakefront lots and the gated Windswept peninsula on Cherokee Lake, are among the named lake communities. When a lake home has a walkout lower level, the concrete walls want closed cell foam, and the rest of the house comes down to attic and rim joist work.

Permits inside and outside the city

City addresses go through the Morristown Building Inspections Department. Unincorporated Hamblen County uses the county Planning Department, which adopted the 2018 residential, building and energy codes on March 24, 2022. Both offices are on the same 2018 cycle, so the insulation minimums line up across the line.

A manufacturing town

Morristown has more than 100 manufacturers, and a 2021 Chamber survey listed Koch Foods, MAHLE Powertrain, Team Technologies and Arconic among its largest employers, alongside Hamblen County Schools and Walters State Community College. Industry on that scale means metal buildings and commercial space, where condensation on bare steel is a real problem.

Most-Requested Services in Morristown

  • Attic insulation: in a city where the median house dates to 1976, the attic is where most homes start, and it is the cheapest place to add depth.
  • Injection foam for existing walls: the 4,668 units from the 1960s and 1970s are worth checking, since injection works in closed, sheathed walls that hold no old cellulose.
  • Crawl space insulation: older floors over vented crawls feel cold in a Morristown January, and sealing the crawl walls fixes that at the source.
  • Air sealing: older homes leak at attic hatches, pipe penetrations and the joint where the walls meet the foundation, and TVA EnergyRight lists a $300 air sealing rebate through its Quality Contractor Network.
  • Closed cell spray foam: for lower-level walls in Cherokee Lake homes and anywhere foam meets concrete.
  • Metal building insulation: closed cell sprayed to the panels keeps steel shops and warehouses from dripping.
  • Commercial spray foam: offices, plants and churches across a city with a deep industrial base.
