Quick Answers

What building code does Lenoir City enforce?

The city's Building Inspection and Permits office enforces the 2021 International Residential Code and the related 2021 codes, with local amendments. Its page does not name an energy code edition, so we confirm the insulation table with the office before pricing new work. Lots outside city limits go to Loudon County Planning and Codes instead.

Do lake houses here need a different kind of foam?

Below grade, yes. The Department of Energy treats closed cell foam as the preferred choice for foundations and basement walls and marks open cell as not acceptable there, since open cell holds water if it gets wet. Above grade, in attics and interior walls, a Lenoir City lake house can use open cell like any other home.

How old are Lenoir City's homes?

The median year built is 1983. Of 4,894 housing units, about 47 percent predate 1980, with 550 from 1939 or earlier and 692 from the 1950s. At the same time, roughly 23.5 percent were built in 2010 or later, including 326 since 2020. The city has an old core and a busy new edge.
