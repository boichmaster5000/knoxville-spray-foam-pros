A typical 1,500 square foot Knoxville home runs $3,000 to $7,500 total. Cost depends on foam type, accessibility, and total square footage being insulated.

Spray foam insulation lasts 80 to 100 years when properly installed, making it a one-time investment for most Knoxville homeowners. Unlike fiberglass batt which compresses and loses R-value over time, spray foam maintains its insulating properties indefinitely.

Closed cell spray foam has an R-value of about 7 per inch and acts as a vapor barrier, making it ideal for crawl spaces, rim joists, and exterior walls. Open cell has an R-value of about 3.5 per inch, is softer and more permeable, and is typically used in attics and interior walls where moisture barrier isn't required.
