What to Look For in a Spray Foam Installer

Before you sign a contract or make a deposit on spray foam work in Knoxville, ask for every item below in writing. A quality installer provides all of them without hesitation. Never sign without them.

  • State contractor license - verify the contractor is properly licensed and registered to operate in Knox County and the State of Tennessee.
  • Proof of insurance - ask for a current certificate of general liability insurance and workers comp coverage, naming you as a certificate holder for the job.
  • SPFA or BPI credentials - the Spray Polyurethane Foam Alliance (SPFA PCP) and the Building Performance Institute (BPI) are the two recognized credentialing bodies for spray foam installers. Ask which the installer or crew holds and request a copy of the current credential.
  • Written warranty terms - get the workmanship warranty (adhesion, coverage, density) and the manufacturer's material warranty in writing before work starts.
  • EPA Lead-Safe (RRP) firm status - if your Knoxville or Oak Ridge home was built before 1978, ask for proof the firm is EPA Lead-Safe certified for renovation, repair, and painting work that disturbs lead paint.
  • Written scope and price - foam type, R-value, square footage, prep work, old-insulation removal, and total cost should all appear on the quote before you commit.

Why We Focus on Knoxville

Knoxville is unique. IECC Climate Zone 4A means humid summers and cold-enough winters that builders have to balance vapor management against R-value. Older Bearden and Oak Ridge homes have low-slope roofs and tight attics. Newer Farragut and West Knoxville builds have generous spaces but were often built to bare-minimum code R-values. Pole barns and metal buildings dot Maryville, Karns, and the rural Knox County edges. We have seen all of it. Every Knoxville neighborhood has its own quirks and we have the local experience to spec the right foam, the right thickness, and the right detail work for each one.
