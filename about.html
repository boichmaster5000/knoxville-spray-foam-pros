About Knoxville Spray Foam Pros

Knoxville Spray Foam Pros is a local insulation contractor serving Knoxville, Tennessee and the surrounding East Tennessee region. We specialize in residential, agricultural, and light commercial spray polyurethane foam installation - including open cell, closed cell, attic, crawl space, metal building, and pole barn applications.

Who We Are

The Knoxville Spray Foam Pros team is a tight crew of trained installers who decided Knoxville homeowners deserved a more honest spray foam company. Too many contractors in East Tennessee subcontract the spray gun out to whoever has a truck that week, and the homeowner ends up with thin coverage and a foam smell that does not quit. We built this company on the opposite principle: one crew, one standard, one warranty.

Our Mission

We exist to make Knoxville homes more comfortable, more efficient, and more durable. The right spray foam install in a Knoxville attic or crawl space cuts utility bills by 30 to 50 percent and pays for itself inside a decade. Done wrong, spray foam can trap moisture, fail to adhere, or leave a chemical odor. We do it right the first time so our customers never have a horror story to tell.

Certifications & Memberships

  • BPI Certified - our installers complete Building Performance Institute training in building science, moisture management, and air sealing
  • SPFA Member - we belong to the Spray Polyurethane Foam Alliance and follow SPFA installation standards
  • Tennessee Licensed - properly licensed and registered to operate in Knox County and the State of Tennessee
  • Fully Insured - general liability and workers comp coverage on every Knoxville job
  • EPA Lead-Safe - certified for older Knoxville and Oak Ridge homes that may contain lead paint

Why We Focus on Knoxville

Knoxville is unique. IECC Climate Zone 4A means humid summers and cold-enough winters that builders have to balance vapor management against R-value. Older Bearden and Oak Ridge homes have low-slope roofs and tight attics. Newer Farragut and West Knoxville builds have generous spaces but were often built to bare-minimum code R-values. Pole barns and metal buildings dot Maryville, Karns, and the rural Knox County edges. We have seen all of it. Every Knoxville neighborhood has its own quirks and we have the local experience to spec the right foam, the right thickness, and the right detail work for each one.
