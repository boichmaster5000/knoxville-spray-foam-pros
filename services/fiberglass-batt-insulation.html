Quick Answers

Which batt goes in a 2x4 or a 2x6 wall?

A 2x4 wall takes an R-13 or R-15 batt, both made 3.5 inches thick to match the stud depth. A 2x6 wall takes R-19 or R-21. Tennessee's statewide code asks for at least R-13 in a wood frame wall, so a properly installed 2x4 batt meets the minimum and a 2x6 wall clears it.

Are batts cheaper than spray foam?

Yes, usually by a wide margin. Fixr's January 2026 insulation guide lists fiberglass batts and rolls at $0.30 to $1.50 per square foot, while 2026 guides put installed open cell foam at $1.50 to $3.50. Batts do nothing to seal air leaks, though, so budget for air sealing as its own step.

Can batts insulate a floor over a crawl space?

Yes, when the crawl space stays vented and dry and the batts stay pressed against the subfloor. Tennessee's code calls for R-19 in floors over unconditioned space. In a damp crawl, water can condense on the cold underside of floor insulation, so we check moisture before recommending batts there.

When Batts Make Sense

Batts need an open cavity and regular framing. These are the jobs where we reach for them:

  • Open stud walls in a room addition, sunroom conversion or gut remodel.
  • Floors over a vented, dry crawl space or an unheated garage.
  • Attic floors with good access, where batts can lie flat at full thickness.
  • The fiber half of a flash and batt wall, behind a thin coat of closed cell foam.

Batts are the wrong tool in closed walls, which call for injection foam for existing walls, and at rim joists, where Building Science Corporation warns that fibrous insulation against the cold band invites condensation.

How Batt Installation Works

A batt slows heat moving through a cavity, but it does little to stop moving air. The Department of Energy's air sealing guide says insulation reduces heat flow but does not block air movement, so every batt job we do starts with sealing.

Seal the framing first

We close gaps at plates, wire and pipe holes, and window and door frames.

3

Cut to fit, never stuff

Every batt runs the full height of its bay and touches both studs, with no gaps or folded edges. At a cable we split the batt around the wire, and we cut around boxes and pipes instead of packing fiberglass behind them.

4

Face it the right way

Kraft facing goes toward the heated side, as Department of Energy guidance says. We never add plastic sheeting behind drywall here, and we never lay one faced batt over another.

Flash and batt for a tighter wall

One manufacturer's data sheet shows a single inch of closed cell foam working as an air barrier at 0.91 perms, and the DOE Building America spray foam guide calls for 1 to 1.5 inches or more in hybrid walls, depending on climate. In a 2x6 wall, 1.5 inches of closed cell at R-6.0 to R-7.4 per inch adds roughly R-9 to R-11, and an R-13 batt fills the rest for a total near R-22 to R-24.

For noise, NAIMA's tests scored a 2x4 wall with R-13 unfaced fiberglass at STC 33, close to foam-filled walls; see soundproofing insulation for bigger gains.

Batts and the East Tennessee Climate

Knox County and its neighbors sit in Climate Zone 4A, with cold winter nights and muggy summers. Tennessee's amended 2018 IECC still uses the 2009 insulation table, so the minimum is R-13 for a wood frame wall and R-19 for a floor.

Local editions differ. The City of Knoxville applies the 2018 IECC, while Maryville moved to the 2024 IECC on January 1, 2026, so fiberglass insulation in a Maryville addition may need to beat the state table. We confirm each address with its codes office.

Building Science Corporation also notes that vented crawl spaces in the South pull in damp air that condenses on floor insulation. If a crawl smells musty or shows wet framing, crawl space wall insulation usually beats floor batts.
