What Is Open Cell Spray Foam?

Open cell spray polyurethane foam (ocSPF) is a low-density (about 0.5 lb per cubic foot) spray-applied foam that expands rapidly when sprayed and cures into a soft, flexible insulation. The cells within the foam are deliberately broken open during the chemical reaction, leaving the foam vapor-permeable, soft to the touch, and excellent at filling irregular cavities. Open cell expands roughly 100 times its liquid volume, completely filling a stud bay or rafter space in a single pass.

Open cell foam was originally developed as a more cost-effective alternative to closed cell for residential framing applications. In Knoxville and across IECC Climate Zone 4A, open cell has become the default choice for attic spray foam because the vapor-permeable nature allows any moisture that does get into the assembly to dry outward through the foam rather than getting trapped.

Benefits of Open Cell Spray Foam in Knoxville

  • Lower cost per square foot - Roughly half the price of closed cell at the same R-value when measured by job cost.
  • Excellent air sealing - Despite being vapor-permeable, open cell is air-impermeable and stops air infiltration across Knoxville framing.
  • Superior sound dampening - The soft cell structure absorbs sound, making it the better choice for interior walls between Knoxville bedrooms and living rooms.
  • Complete cavity fill - One-pass expansion gets into every gap, around wiring, and behind plumbing.
  • Vapor permeability - Allows moisture to dry outward in attics, which suits Knoxville Climate Zone 4A.
  • Long lifespan - Like closed cell, open cell lasts 80 to 100 years with no R-value loss.
  • Reduces HVAC sizing - Air sealing cuts cooling and heating loads enough that smaller equipment is often viable on Knoxville retrofits.

Our Open Cell Spray Foam Process in Knoxville

  1. Site visit & measurement. An estimator measures the attic or wall space, identifies existing insulation, and notes any prep work needed.
  2. Old insulation removal. Existing fiberglass batt or blown-in cellulose is removed and bagged for disposal.
  3. Substrate prep. We mask HVAC returns, cover floors below the attic hatch, and confirm substrate temperature.
  4. Equipment warm-up. The proportioner heats both A and B chemicals to spec. Open cell ratio is critical for proper expansion.
  5. Spray application. Foam is sprayed in a single pass at the specified thickness - typically 5 to 14 inches in a Knoxville attic for R-19 to R-49.
  6. Trim and inspect. Over-expanded areas are trimmed flush with framing for drywall clearance.
  7. Cleanup and walkthrough. We remove masking, photograph coverage, and walk the homeowner through the completed work.

Knoxville-Specific Considerations

Knoxville and the surrounding East Tennessee region sit in IECC Climate Zone 4A. The 2021 IECC recommends R-49 in attics for this zone. Open cell foam achieves this at about 14 inches, which fits comfortably in a typical 2x12 rafter bay or in an unvented attic conversion. The vapor-permeable nature of open cell is a feature, not a bug, in Knoxville - if a small roof leak develops, water can dry through the foam rather than getting trapped against the deck. TVA EnergyRight rebates and the federal IRA tax credit apply to qualifying open cell installs in Knoxville. We pull all available paperwork during the quote.
