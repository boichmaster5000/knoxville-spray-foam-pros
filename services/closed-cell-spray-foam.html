What Is Closed Cell Spray Foam?

Closed cell spray polyurethane foam (ccSPF) is a two-component spray-applied insulation that expands rapidly when sprayed and cures into a dense, rigid plastic. The "closed cell" refers to the structure of the foam: each microscopic cell is sealed, holding a low-conductivity gas that gives the foam its high R-value. A 2-inch lift of closed cell foam delivers an R-value of about 14, qualifies as a Class II vapor retarder, and adds measurable racking strength to the wall or roof deck it is sprayed on.

Closed cell foam was originally developed for industrial applications. Over the last 25 years it has become a standard product for high-performance residential and light commercial construction. In Knoxville, where humidity is a real factor much of the year, closed cell foam is the go-to product anywhere moisture management matters.

Benefits of Closed Cell Spray Foam in Knoxville

  • Highest R-value per inch - R-7 per inch means you hit code R-values in less depth, which matters in tight Knoxville crawl spaces and shallow rim joist cavities.
  • Built-in vapor barrier - At 2 inches or more, closed cell qualifies as a Class II vapor retarder, blocking the humid East Tennessee air from condensing inside your assembly.
  • Structural reinforcement - Sprayed against sheathing, closed cell adds measurable racking strength and stiffness, which matters for older Knoxville framing.
  • Stops bulk water intrusion - Closed cell foam does not absorb water, making it suitable for below-grade walls and damp crawl spaces.
  • Long lifespan - Properly applied closed cell foam lasts 80 to 100 years with no degradation, making it a one-time install for Knoxville homeowners.
  • Air sealing - Foam seals every penetration, gap, and crack, cutting Knoxville home air infiltration by 40 percent or more on blower-door tests.
  • Reduces HVAC sizing - A tighter, better-insulated Knoxville home can run on a smaller AC and furnace, saving thousands on equipment replacement.
  • Rejects pests and rodents - Closed cell foam offers no food value and is too dense for typical pests to tunnel through.

Our Closed Cell Spray Foam Process in Knoxville

  1. Assessment & quote. An estimator visits your Knoxville home, measures the space, takes moisture readings, and identifies any prep work needed before spraying.
  2. Site prep. We mask off adjacent surfaces, cover floors, and seal HVAC returns to prevent overspray. Power and water are confirmed.
  3. Equipment setup. The proportioner and heated hoses come up to spec temperature - critical for proper closed cell ratio.
  4. Substrate inspection. We confirm substrate temperature, dryness, and cleanliness before pulling the trigger.
  5. Spray application. Closed cell foam is sprayed in passes (lifts) of 1.5 to 2 inches, allowing each pass to cool before the next.
  6. Trim & inspect. Any over-thickness areas are trimmed back, depth is verified at multiple points, and coverage is photographed.
  7. Cure & cleanup. The space cures 24 hours before the homeowner returns. We remove all masking, drop cloths, and waste.

Knoxville-Specific Considerations

Knoxville is in IECC Climate Zone 4A - mixed-humid. Summers regularly hit 90°F+ with dew points in the 70s. Winters drop below freezing 30+ nights per year. This combination makes closed cell foam particularly valuable in Knoxville because it stops both summer moisture drive into the assembly and winter heat loss. The vapor barrier nature of closed cell is critical for crawl spaces under older Bearden, Fountain City, and downtown Knoxville homes where dirt floors and high water tables drive constant moisture upward. Closed cell sprayed on the underside of the subfloor or on the crawl walls cuts that moisture path.
