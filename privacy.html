Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens to the information you share with Knoxville Spray Foam Pros through this website, by phone or by email. Last updated October 22, 2026.

What the Estimate Form Collects

  • Your name.
  • Your phone number.
  • What you tell us you need insulated, plus anything else you choose to type in the message box.

You never have to use the form. You can call (865) 564-7823 or email info@knoxvillesprayfoampros.com instead.

How the Form Works

The form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. When you press the submit button, your entries go to Formspree, which delivers them to us by email and keeps a copy in its system under its own privacy policy. The form includes a hidden spam filter field and then sends you to a thank-you page on this site.

Who Receives Your Information

Inquiries from this website, by form, phone or email, go to the local insulation contractor who performs the work, so they can contact you and set up your free estimate. We use your details only to respond to your request, schedule the estimate and carry out any work you hire us for. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Phone Calls and Email

Calls to (865) 564-7823 are routed through a phone service provider, which may keep basic call records such as the calling number, date and time. Emails to info@knoxvillesprayfoampros.com are kept in our mailbox for as long as we need them to handle your project.

Cookies and Analytics

This site does not currently run advertising trackers or third-party analytics. If that changes, this page will be updated to say what is collected and how to opt out.

Deleting Your Information

To ask us to delete what you sent, email info@knoxvillesprayfoampros.com or call (865) 564-7823 and tell us the phone number you used. We will remove it from our records. Formspree's copy is handled under Formspree's own policy, and we will delete the submission from our Formspree account as well.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
