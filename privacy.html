Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary of what Knoxville Spray Foam Pros does with the information you share through the quote form on this site or when you call (865) 564-7823. Last updated October 2026.

What the quote form collects

  • Your first and last name.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your email address (optional on some forms).
  • Your property address, which may include city or ZIP code.
  • The service type you selected and any comments you added.

You do not need to submit the form to get a quote - you can also call (865) 564-7823 directly and share the same details by phone.

How your information is used

The information you share through the form is sent to an independent local spray foam professional in the Knoxville area so they can respond to your request with a quote. It is used to understand what you are insulating, where the property is, and how to reach you with a quote. Nothing is sold to third-party marketing lists.

Who sees your information

  • The independent local spray foam professional handling your request.
  • The form delivery service that transports the submission from the website to that professional.
  • No one else - we do not share your details with unrelated advertisers or data brokers.

How to ask for deletion

If you want the information you submitted deleted, call (865) 564-7823 during business hours (Mon-Sat, 7AM to 7PM) and ask for your inquiry to be removed. Please have the phone number or address you submitted ready so we can locate the record. We handle deletion requests over the phone and do not require you to send an email.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently run any third-party analytics or advertising scripts. If that changes, this page will be updated to describe what is tracked and how to opt out.

Phone calls and text messages

If you call or text (865) 564-7823, your phone number and the content of the conversation may be stored by the independent local spray foam professional for the duration of your project and any follow-up. You can ask for your number to be removed from their list at any time.

Changes to this policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. For questions about this policy, call (865) 564-7823.
